Copy this into your usual survey or give it out on paper. No name or account
needed. Return privately to the facilitator; do not post learner responses in
public issues. Do not include personal, patient, or workplace data.

Facilitator fills in: date ___ · workshop commit/tag ___ · runtime ___

1. The pace was: too slow 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 too fast. (3 is about right.)
2. How much did you enjoy it? not at all 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 a lot
3. Which can you explain now? Check any: axis meaning / broadcasting / contraction / rank budget / none yet.
4. Where did you get stuck? Notebook and heading: ___
5. What changed your mind? Name one prediction and the evidence: ___
6. What should we change? Shorten ___ / add an example of ___ / allow more time for ___

Facilitator note: record actual section times on the [day sheet's timing
strip](day-sheet.md#timing-strip), the median of the two ratings, and
anonymous counts of common errors. A pace median away from 3 says more about
the next run's timings than any single comment.
Open one concrete issue for the next run. Keep raw responses private.
