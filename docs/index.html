Tensors for Machine Learning

A 3-hour workshop with code, real data, and three quizzes

The whole day in one picture Video overview One-minute shorts Infographics Self-check quiz Mind map In NotebookLM

What this is

Learn to reshape, solve, convolve, and factorize tensors in 210 minutes.

Work with images, taxi trips, and airline counts. Find missing values, uneven scales, and constant pixels. Small synthetic examples explain selected ideas.

Tensors in machine learning — video overview — Watch in NotebookLM.

Who it is for

You know the linear algebra in Deep Learning (Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville), Chapter 2 — Linear Algebra. No tensor theory is required. New terms are explained as they appear.

Prerequisites

The tools are free and run in your browser.

Do before the session

  • Review the linear algebra in Deep Learning (Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville), Chapter 2 — Linear Algebra: vectors, matrices, products, inverses, eigendecomposition, and SVD.
  • linear-algebra-deep-learning — required pre-work. Complete 14 bilingual notebooks, from vectors to PCA. Allow 2–3 hours.
  • Notebook 00, run in Colab — check setup and data downloads. Report any errors before the session.

Recommended

  • Essence of linear algebra (3Blue1Brown) — a visual refresher. 16 videos, about 3 hours.

Have ready on the day

  • Colab for coding.
  • Discord for questions and group work. The facilitator shares the invite.
  • A phone or second tab for Kahoot. Join with the session PIN.

How we work

  • Exercise blocks — 10 minutes coding, then 5 minutes explanation.
  • Group blocks — 10 minutes discussion in your breakout channel, then share-back.

A note on language

Teaching is in English. Ask questions in English or Spanish. The slides and notebooks support both languages.
